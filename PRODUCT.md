# KeenKids Enrichment — Product

## What it is
After-school STEAM enrichment for grades 1–5 (ages 6–11) run on elementary school campuses in Milpitas, California. Founded by Anjana Venkat and Shilpa Javli, two Silicon Valley technology professionals and parents.

Kids build things with their hands, run science experiments, code with Scratch, and practise public speaking, storytelling, improv and debate, with free-exploration time alongside. A new build theme runs every month (August–May).

## Who the site serves
1. **Parents of elementary-school children** at participating schools (Mabel Mattos, John Sinnott, Rose, Curtner). Busy, often reading on a phone between pickups. Their questions: what will my child do, will they enjoy it, are they safe, what does it cost, where and when, how do I enroll.
2. **Schools, PTAs and parents at other schools** who might want to host KeenKids.
3. **Prospective instructors** (part-time, after-school hours).

## Mode
Persuade (homepage and enrollment journey), with Read surfaces for FAQ, pricing and policy pages.

## Primary action
Find your school → open that school's real enrollment or interest form (Google Forms). Secondary: ask a question (email).

## Proof available
Four real program photographs (faces softened), twelve real parent testimonials, the founders' real story and photographs, the real monthly curriculum, real pricing and policies. Nothing else may be claimed.

- Payment links (Stripe) are preserved but guarded in the preview; Parent Portal link preserved unchanged.
